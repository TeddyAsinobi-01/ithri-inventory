Send Report to WhatsApp

Builds one clear image of everything entered today — what each area started with, what was used, and what's left.

When the report has been sent, close the day. Today's report is saved to the database and today's closing figures become tomorrow's opening stock.

If you forget: any device that has the site open at 11:59 PM closes the day for you, and saves the report. If nobody has the site open at that time, the day is closed the first moment anyone opens the site afterwards. Either way nothing is lost, and nothing is closed on a day with no entries.
